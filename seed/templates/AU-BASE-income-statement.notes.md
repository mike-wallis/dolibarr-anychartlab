**Australia: multi-step income statement with gross profit.**

Small proprietary companies in Australia usually prepare *special purpose* financial reports, and
there is no single mandatory layout for the profit and loss. This template follows the common
multi-step layout used by accountants and accounting software:

- **Revenue**, less **Cost of sales** = **Gross profit**.
- **Other income** (interest, grants, gains, anything not trading revenue).
- **Expenses**: employment expenses (wages, superannuation), operating expenses, other expenses.
- **Net profit before tax**, less **Income tax expense** = **Net profit after tax**.

Things to know:

- Amounts exclude GST. GST collected and paid are balance sheet accounts, not income or expenses.
- Superannuation guarantee is an employment expense.
- Charts of accounts vary a lot in Australia (there is no national chart), so check the account
  prefixes in each step against your own chart. The defaults assume revenue in 4xxx, cost of sales
  in 5xxx, expenses in 6xxx and income tax in 99xx.
- General purpose reports (larger or reporting entities) follow AASB 101, which may need a
  different presentation.
