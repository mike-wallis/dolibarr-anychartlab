**Generic income statement: income less expenses.**

Use it when there is no template for your country, or to start very simply and add your own groups.

- **Income**: every account classified as income.
- **Expenses**: every account classified as expense.
- **Net result** = Income − Expenses.

Most countries require a more detailed layout for statutory accounts (for example a gross profit
line, or operating / financial / exceptional sections). Add groups and formulas in the wizard to
match what your country requires.

*Written 2026-10-12. Review status: generic (not tied to any country's rules).*
